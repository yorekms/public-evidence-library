# Earlier energy and raw-material histories — 1 November 2026

This additive extension preserves the complete earlier public collection. It adds 1,096 source-defined measures and 468,915 retained source cells. The current catalogue contains 9,852 measures in 34 collections, with 14,322,061 retained normalized records. Unavailable cells and source symbols remain records; they are not counted as measured zeros.

## Energy through earlier source years

- Historical country energy production: seven native fuel series for 1900–1979, from Etemad and Luciani through The Shift Project. Original historical entities and aggregate accounts have separate source-defined histories. The seven fuel series retain Mtoe source units. Repeated country/fuel/year keys remain unavailable, with every alternative source row attached.
- EIA international energy: annual country production, consumption and related physical measures, with overall coverage 1973–2025; most countries begin in 1980 or later. Native whole-country identifiers are required for country maps. Offshore accounts, historical areas, territories without an exact whole-country identity, regions and aggregates stay in separate source-defined histories.
- U.S. energy production and consumption: 25 EIA Monthly Energy Review annual series, 1949–2025. These are publisher annual totals, not locally summed monthly data.
- U.S. crude-oil field production: two distinct native-unit series, 1859–2025, retained from the original EIA bulk records. They are not added together.
- Historical U.S. benchmarks: nine separate sparse series for selected publisher years, 1635–1945. Early fuelwood entries include publisher-assigned decade-average estimates. They are not continuous annual measurements and are not assigned to current country polygons. Geographic coverage and historical definitions change; the original notes remain attached.

Energy source guide and original downloads: [/data/EARLY_ENERGY.md](/data/EARLY_ENERGY.md).

Energy accounting and checksums: [/data/early-energy-manifest.json](/data/early-energy-manifest.json).

The historical Shift branch retains CC BY-SA 3.0 attribution to Etemad and Luciani and The Shift Project. EIA branches retain their separate public-domain source attribution. Different sources and methods are not spliced into one supposedly comparable series.

## Raw materials, 1900–2023 overall

All 92 original public-domain USGS Data Series 140 commodity workbooks are retained. They supply 990 native column histories: 895 United States series and 95 separate publisher world-total series. Individual commodity and column coverage varies.

The United States series include native production, supply, demand, imports, exports, use and unit-value columns where the source provides them. Physical basis, native units, nominal prices and source constant-dollar values remain distinct. No local price adjustment, unit conversion or formula recalculation was performed. Cached workbook formula values, literal cells, source addresses, headers, comments and table notes remain attached.

World totals have geography `world_aggregate`. They are available in the complete catalogue and source-history views, and never provide values to individual country polygons. Source NA, withheld W, blanks and ambiguous duplicate-year entries stay unavailable. A numeric source zero remains zero.

## Maps and complete histories

The raised map opens north-up with a high viewing angle matching the supplied appearance reference: tilt 80 degrees, rotation -90 degrees, original source-height emphasis, and a larger opening zoom on wide maps. Narrow maps retain fitted zoom so their world overview stays usable. Existing tilt, rotation, height, zoom, flat-map, timeline and playback controls remain available.

Histories exceeding 80 calendar years load bounded drawing windows. Their color, height and camera-fit domains remain fixed across the complete retained history. Earlier observations are not trimmed. Missing windows remain unavailable, and all original source records remain accessible through the source table and complete-history CSV.

Use World maps for actual country series. Use the full catalogue's geography selector for World totals (publisher aggregates) or Historical entities and source-defined places, then open a measure to inspect its source history. Sparse benchmark years remain discrete source points; no missing annual observations are constructed.
