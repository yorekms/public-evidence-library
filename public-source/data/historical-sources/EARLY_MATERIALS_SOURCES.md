# Earlier material histories: USGS Data Series140

92 original public-domain commodity workbooks are preserved, including their embedded notes and source formatting. 990 native annual column series add 107580 cells spanning 1900–2023. Coverage varies by commodity and column.

United States observations and publisher world-production totals are separate datasets. World totals are source aggregates and never supply values to country polygons. Production, trade, stocks, consumption and physical or monetary unit values remain separate columns. Nominal and1998-dollar unit values retain the publisher adjustments; nothing is recalculated.

Typed numeric cells and publisher-cached numeric formula values are extracted. Every included annual cell keeps its workbook, sheet, cell address, literal header, native number format, original value, formula/cache and source comment. Blank, NA, W and other nonnumeric source cells stay unavailable. The series includes all native table notes and prelude; full XLSX bytes, omitted nonnumeric-only columns, embedded notes/objects and every original worksheet remain downloadable in the archive.

Original source: [https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/historical-statistics-mineral-commodities-united](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/historical-statistics-mineral-commodities-united). Source workbook hashes, member hashes, units, table notes, coverage, row accounting and packet checksums are in[the manifest](/data/early-materials-manifest.json). The[original-source archive](/data/historical-sources/early-materials-usgs-ds140-originals.zip) preserves the downloaded source bytes.
