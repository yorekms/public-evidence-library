# Learning first, with a cumulative economic research foundation

Prepared 31 October 2026. This is an additive project design and implementation record. It preserves the seven existing Civic Voice chapters, the complete local research workbench, original source files and prior editions. Proposed research designs below are not fitted results or demonstrated learning effects.

The primary purpose is to help people understand their world: find a question, inspect relevant evidence, compare plausible explanations, and reconsider what they think. An answer is optional. Public information should remain available regardless of whether a person guesses, signs in, writes a reflection or consents to contribute research data.

**One evidence foundation, three connected uses**

| Part | Purpose | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Public Evidence Library | Open questions, definitions, source catalogue and selected interactive histories; complete original-source references | Reading is open. New-library notes remain in the browser and can be downloaded; no answers are uploaded. |
| Civic Voice | Existing guided chapters, optional consented private learning-review saves, separate poll responses and organizer export | Its existing audience and account/organizer rules remain. A learning review is not a vote. New question-card notes are not silently accepted by the seven-chapter backend. |
| Personal research workbench | Full retained datasets, source-linked maps, deterministic statistics, monetary accounts, explicit scenarios and future economic investigations | Preserve native measurement populations, uncertainty, assumptions and full reproducible records. No interface-selected pattern alone establishes causality. |

The first public catalogue indexes 7,811 built-in measures in 22 numerical collections, representing 13,431,313 retained rows in the local edition. The public source-view edition currently contains 290 measures and 104,483 retained rows. Its county examples retain Virginia/Iowa source records; the world-food examples use an explicitly selected set of named reporting economies. These are declared public display subsets, not substitutes for the full original source. Source and coverage metadata remain beside the values, and the larger original downloads remain linked. Other pipeline, ownership, facility and global-account source inventories remain available through the public source index.

**A learning sequence that does not impose a conclusion**

1. Ask a specific question with a defined place, time, quantity and population.
2. Offer an optional expectation and confidence, or let the reader explore directly.
3. Show the source-defined numbers, date, unit, missingness and uncertainty. Preserve source estimates and flags.
4. Ask what pattern the reader sees, what could explain it, and what competing explanation remains plausible.
5. Offer reflection: changed, unchanged or uncertain; ask what evidence might change the view and why.
6. Keep any choice about sharing a research response separate from access to the material.

An empirical result can contradict a factual expectation when the measure and comparison actually answer that expectation. A preference or policy priority does not become incorrect because it differs from an organizer's view. No automated political answer key or blanket learning score is introduced. “I need more evidence” is a useful response.

Question wording, options and order need testing with readers before treating a response instrument as validated. AAPOR recommends clear, neutral questions and adequate response choices, including uncertainty when appropriate. It also recommends considering whether existing administrative/public data can answer the research question without a survey. [AAPOR best practices](https://aapor.org/standards-and-ethics/best-practices/).

**Keep the research streams distinct**

Public data answer questions about measured production, income, emissions, prices or institutions within their source definitions. Participant responses describe what consenting participants report believing, noticing or prioritizing. The second stream must not become evidence that the first stream's economic explanation is correct. Likewise, an observed country difference does not explain why a particular participant answered as they did.

Volunteer website participation is not a probability sample of a state or country. Disclose recruitment, exact question wording/order, source edition, participation and missingness rather than claiming a population margin of error. AAPOR's transparency guidance calls for documenting how participants were selected, including opt-in methods. [AAPOR transparency guidance](https://aapor.org/standards-and-ethics/transparency-initiative/).

The existing chapter backend saves one current review per account/chapter/source edition/selection. Saving again updates that record. It does not retain a longitudinal history of revisions. The generic local explorer has confidence fields, but the current seven-chapter backend does not accept those fields. These are existing contracts to preserve, not features to assume already exist.

For a future versioned research-response service, the proposed record should include a question/instrument version, explicit consent version, source edition and fingerprints, native selected place/time IDs, optional expectation/confidence, explicit reveal/skip status, reflection, and server receipt time. Keep client event times as client reports. If repeated change is a research objective, append revisions/events rather than replacing the previous answer; declare retention/deletion and participant/organizer access before collecting. Pseudonymous account identifiers and private text belong outside public aggregates and public source downloads.

A before/after self-report is descriptive. It cannot by itself establish that this interface caused learning. A causal learning study would need a declared comparison or randomized design, exposure/comprehension checks and a prespecified analysis. That would be a separate research addition.

**Objective assessment means inspectable choices**

The achievable standard is reproducible calculation and transparent judgment. Choosing an outcome, denominator, model, missingness rule or welfare criterion still requires decisions. Preserve those decisions so another analyst can reproduce or challenge them.

For each proposed economic conjecture, save a research record with:

- The exact question, proposed mechanism and a competing explanation.
- Outcome, unit of analysis, population, geographic identity and native time resolution.
- Original source editions/hashes, joins/crosswalk vintages, revisions and unresolved identities.
- Inclusion/exclusion rules, units, transformations, denominators, missingness and reporting coverage.
- The model/equation, assumptions, information cutoff and benchmark.
- A comparison/counterfactual and the assumptions required for any causal interpretation.
- Validation, uncertainty and sensitivity checks, including a held-out period when appropriate.
- Results and failed predictions, source-linked figures and conditions that would change the conclusion.

Do not silently remove inconvenient values, treat unknown ownership as zero, blend stocks and flows, add overlapping commodity classes, or infer a shipment link from proximity. A stable-panel analysis and a changing-source-universe total answer different questions. Keep both available with their row accounting.

**Six concrete research starters**

| Proposed question | Existing foundation | Next design/evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Where does greater income translate into greater purchasing power or lower housing pressure? | BEA regional prices/real income, household-income and rent-burden measures | Compatible household populations and years. Avoid using a price-deflated measure's mathematical relationship with its own deflator as independent economic evidence. |
| When resource production changes, where do employment, wages and public revenue change? | NASS food histories, EIA state production, reported facilities, fiscal comparisons | Consistent county/industry employment and wages, local fiscal data, prices, population and comparison units. A state total cannot be assigned to a mine's community. |
| Does a change in ownership precede a change in production or reported environmental load? | Dated ownership/operator records, facilities and program reporting | Actual effective dates, verified asset identity, shares/coverage, matched periods, pre-trends, stable reporters and alternatives to an ownership effect. Inventory counts are not market shares. |
| How do producer and consumer emissions accounts differ, and what economic relationships accompany that gap? | Official OECD footprint histories plus separately dated monetary accounts | Compatible years, non-overlapping economies/aggregates, residence/accounting boundaries and uncertainty. National value added does not identify a household's benefit or a firm's profit. |
| Do trade-policy changes predict a different pattern of commodity trade? | Tariff/governance indicators, selected customs flows and policy references | Product/partner-specific measures, HS concordances, a longer multi-reporter panel, timing, confounders and a defensible identification strategy. A national average tariff is not a shipment's legal rate. |
| Which model predicts outcomes beyond its fitting period? | Existing input–output accounting and statistical workbench | Competing models with different predictions, common information sets, held-out observations, benchmarks, uncertainty and explicit failure criteria. Accounting identities are not independent predictive tests. |

The input–output demand model remains conditional on fixed source-year input shares; it does not solve prices, substitution, capacity or household behavior. The previously unreconciled environmental demand extension remains unavailable. No new model or conjecture is declared supported merely because a public question card exists.

**Build in preserved, reviewable increments**

- **Current public reading edition:** complete catalogue, 14 neutral starter questions, selected native histories, source downloads, private local notes and a visible Civic Voice link.
- **Broader public evidence views:** add versioned static graphs/maps/source tables from the existing pipeline, ownership and environmental records. Keep each published subset and its provenance explicit.
- **Versioned research responses:** extend the existing instrument only after defining consent, question/evidence bindings, confidence semantics, append-versus-update behavior and export/deletion rules. Test participant/organizer separation with real independent accounts before claiming it verified.
- **Economic research projects:** add one declared question and reproducible analysis record at a time. Prioritize compatible local employment/wages, price measures, longer bilateral resource histories and dated control evidence where they resolve a specific design gap.
- **Publication and retention:** preserve complete originals, previous editions, source/row checks, immutable published snapshots and retrievable code. Add summaries and public views as companions rather than replacing the full archive.

This makes the public material useful now while leaving the complete personal research environment available for deeper statistics and later conjecture testing.
