Define the analysis

What do you want to assess?

Lag and first differences require annual time data. A lag is measured in calendar years; missing years are not silently shifted together.

Bayesian prior assumptions

Priors apply to sample-standardized X and Y. The mean sets the standardized slope’s prior mean; the intercept mean is zero. The scale controls coefficient spread, and a/b define the inverse-gamma error-variance prior. Review the exact parameterization in the analysis record.

An observational assessment.

Association depends on source selection, dates, geography, missing data and model assumptions. Source-estimate sampling error is not automatically propagated into the regression. A fitted association does not by itself identify a causal effect.

Keep an assessable research record
  1. State the question and distinguish exploratory work from a planned test.
  2. Identify the measurement population, geography, period and unit.
  3. Inspect missingness and the observations retained by a join.
  4. Assess trends, dependence, residual patterns and sensitivity to assumptions.
  5. Retain the source hash, chosen model, uncertainty definition and limitations when exporting a figure.
Shared source code and a reproducible record make decisions inspectable. A statistical method cannot remove all judgement from the selection and interpretation of data.
